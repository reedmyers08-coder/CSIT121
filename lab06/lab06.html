The website I am judging is Deltarune.com.

The site's target audience is fans of RPGs, meta storytelling, bullet-hells, and of Undertale.

I am included in that group.
Deltarune is great you should play it.

...The website's organization depends on what you consider to be part of the website.

The main page of the website has everything you would need if you were considering buying the game: Descriptions of features, links to store pages, a free demo.
But there's also a bunch of hidden pages that have easter eggs and lore for the game, along with teasers for upcoming content, that range in difficulty to find from vaguely hidden links, to links that take you to pages that don't exist, that you have to rearrange the letters of to find the actual page. (Can you tell I like this game?)

I think the main design principle used is color.

Practically the entire page is either shades of blue, black or white, giving a shadowy feel to the site.

The Audit score for the website was 89%. It seems like it's just complaining about using h5 for sizing.

This site is very effective. It tells you about the game, and shows you where to go to get it.

It is very efficient, as all the relevant links are on the main page.

I find it very pleasant to use, and it is appropriate for a game web page.

My recommendation to make this website better is...

Nothing! I have nothing that I would change.
